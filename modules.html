For practitioners

Concrete structures for recurring governance work.

The modules package repeatable operating routines for intake, prioritization, AI-assisted artifact review, authorization, readiness, controls, and value follow-through. Use them to inspect structure, not as a substitute for the experience evidence or proof standard.

What they are

They are public proof-of-concept packages and workflow examples, not production SaaS products, client operating procedures, or evidence of software engineering ownership.

Workflow chain

The modules follow an operating lifecycle.

01 Intake Capture demand, ownership, and signal quality. 02 Prioritize Compare tradeoffs, capacity, sequence, and decisions. 03 Authorize Turn intent into investment logic and chartered work. 04 Validate Check readiness, signoffs, exposure, and evidence gaps. 05 Realize Track benefits, confidence, outcomes, and follow-through.

AI workflow governance modules work as a review layer across the chain when an idea, artifact, or decision path depends on AI-assisted work.
