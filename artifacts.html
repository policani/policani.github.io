Artifact purpose

This is the proof layer between narrative and repositories.

The artifact library translates real operating experience into generalized frameworks, operating models, playbooks, templates, lessons learned, decision patterns, and public-safe examples. It is designed to show the problem type, intervention logic, evidence standard, and capability demonstrated.

Public-safe by design

The artifacts avoid employer names, client names, logos, screenshots, exact dates, confidential figures, internal systems, and proprietary process detail. The useful operating judgment stays visible without exposing sensitive context.
