Worked example · T-Mobile Revenue Technology · 2021–2024

A ~500-initiative portfolio, reset from 340 to 120 days.

Revenue-critical work tracked through 75+ inconsistent fields with no standard lifecycle. A material share of “active” work was unfunded, under-defined, or stalled — artificial congestion that hid real capacity and stretched delivery cycles to ~340 days. The constraint was distorted work-in-progress and weak readiness discipline, not execution capability.

Maintain the structure

Avoids disruption, but sustains the distortion and the reactive reprioritization.

Re-platform the tooling

Structural consistency, at the cost of delay and adoption resistance.

Reset signal in place — chosen

Reclassify work, normalize lifecycle definitions, and separate qualification from execution — without replacing the platform.

What I put in place

Reviewed ~500 initiatives and reclassified stalled or under-defined work out of the active pipeline. Normalized 75+ tracking fields into one lifecycle with staged readiness gates from concept to launch. Ran a dual-rail model so work entered delivery only when build-ready, and shifted executive reporting to change-based discussions of risk and sequencing rather than status reconciliation.

The outcome

A 65% reduction in average delivery cycle time — from ~340 days to ~120 — across roughly 500 initiatives, with 75+ tracking fields normalized into a single lifecycle and no platform replacement.
