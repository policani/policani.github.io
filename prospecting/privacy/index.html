Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

The Claim Access Directory is an independent project run by Marco Policani in Washington State. This policy covers this website and its two sign-up forms.

What we collect

Prospector sign-up form: your email address, home state, experience level, and the interests you check.

Owner form: your name, email address, state, county, type of ground, number of claims or acres if you give it, how you’d like to be contacted, your phone number if you choose a call, and any notes you add.

Both forms also record that you agreed to be contacted. We don’t collect anything else about you.

How we use it

  • Prospectors get one email when their state opens. We won’t send other mail unless you ask for it.
  • If you told us about your ground, we contact you the way you asked, to talk about listing it.

We never publish your name, email, phone number, or mailing address on this site, and we never sell or rent your information.

Where it goes

Forms are delivered by Web3Forms, which sends each submission to our email inbox and keeps a copy in its own records for 30 days. After that, your details are kept only in our private records, never in the website itself.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, its servers receive your IP address and browser details in order to deliver pages.

How long we keep it

We delete form details 12 months after our last contact with you, unless you’re an owner with a listing on the site. Listing owners’ records are kept while the listing is active and deleted 12 months after it ends.

To have your details deleted sooner, reply to any email we’ve sent you and ask. We’ll delete them within 30 days.

Visitor counts and cookies

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which reports page views and page load times. It doesn’t use cookies or store anything in your browser, and it doesn’t track you across other sites. This site sets no cookies of its own.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top of the page.

Claim Access Directory is an independent project based in Washington State. It is not affiliated with the Bureau of Land Management, the Forest Service, or the Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife.
