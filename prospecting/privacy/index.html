Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

The Claim Access Directory is an independent project run by Marco Policani in Washington State. This policy covers this website and its two forms.

What we collect

Prospector form: your home state, experience level, and the interests you check. It doesn’t ask for your name, email, or any other contact detail.

Owner form: your state, county, type of ground, claim serial numbers or parcel number if you give them, number of claims or acres if you give it, any notes you add, and your confirmation that you own or control the ground. It doesn’t ask for your name, email, or phone number.

We don’t collect anything else about you, and this site never sends email.

How we use it

  • Prospector answers are counted to decide which states open next.
  • If an owner gives claim serial numbers or a parcel number, we mail one letter with a listing code to the mailing address on that public BLM or county record when listings open in that state. We don’t contact owners any other way.

We never sell or rent your information.

Where it goes

Forms are delivered by Web3Forms, which sends each submission to our inbox and keeps a copy in its own records for 30 days. After that, submissions are kept only in our private records, never in the website itself.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, its servers receive your IP address and browser details in order to deliver pages.

How long we keep it

We delete form submissions 12 months after we receive them, unless they belong to an owner with a listing on the site. Listing records are kept while the listing is active and deleted 12 months after it ends.

To have an owner submission deleted sooner, send the owner form again with the same claim or parcel numbers and the word “remove” in the notes. We’ll delete it within 30 days.

Visitor counts and cookies

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which reports page views and page load times. It doesn’t use cookies or store anything in your browser, and it doesn’t track you across other sites. This site sets no cookies of its own.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top of the page.

Claim Access Directory is an independent project based in Washington State. It is not affiliated with the Bureau of Land Management, the Forest Service, or the Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife.
