Prospecting the wrong ground is easy to do

Open land may be claimed

Public land that looks empty can be covered by someone's mining claim, and markers go missing.

Claim jumping is serious

Taking gold or gems from another person's claim without permission can bring trespass claims, and in some cases charges.

Stream rules change by place and season

In Washington, the water body and the time of year decide when and how you may work in a stream.

What the directory will show you

Current claim status

Claim locations and status from Bureau of Land Management records, refreshed every week, on a map you can use from your phone.

Plain-language rules

For each area: who manages the land, which hand methods are allowed, the stream work windows, and what to know about safety and artifacts.

Owners who say yes

Listings from claim owners, clubs, and landowners who have confirmed they control the ground, each with the owner's own way to arrange a visit.

Who it's for

Prospectors

Panners, metal detectorists, rockhounds, and families who want to spend the day digging instead of wondering whose ground they're on.

Claim owners and landowners

Owners of mining claims, prospecting clubs, and private landowners who are open to visitors on their own terms, and want to be found by people who respect the rules.

How verification protects owners

A listing only says "verified" after the owner proves they control the ground. That keeps anyone from listing a claim that isn't theirs.

  1. You tell us about your ground. Claim serial numbers, or the parcel for private land, plus your rules.
  2. We mail a code to the address on the official record. That's the address on the BLM claim record or the county record, not one supplied online.
  3. You enter the code and your listing goes live. You decide the methods allowed, the season, and group size.
  4. We keep checking. If a claim's status changes in BLM records, its listing is hidden until it's resolved.

Your name and mailing address are never shown on the site. Your listing shows only the contact you choose for arranging visits.

What the directory doesn't do

  • It doesn't sell access, take bookings, or handle payments.
  • Owners decide who visits and on what terms. You arrange permission with the owner directly.
  • It can't promise access, and nobody can promise finds.
