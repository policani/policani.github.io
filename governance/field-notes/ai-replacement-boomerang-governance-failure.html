The AI replacement boomerang is a governance failure

AI-driven cuts made without redesigning the work return as rework, lost knowledge, and a broken capability pipeline.

Two-minute briefing A concise companion to the full white paper.

TL;DR

AI-driven cuts made without redesigning the work return as rework, lost knowledge, and a broken capability pipeline.

The operating move

Do not book labor savings until the work, exception path, knowledge transfer, and capability pipeline have been redesigned. Removing people without redesigning the system simply moves cost into rework and fragility.
