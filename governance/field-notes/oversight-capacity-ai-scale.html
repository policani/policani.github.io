AI operating governance · Field note

Oversight capacity, not model capability, is the ceiling on AI scale

Human attention available to review, correct, and answer for AI output is the real scaling constraint.

Two-minute briefing A concise companion to the full white paper.

TL;DR

Human attention available to review, correct, and answer for AI output is the real scaling constraint.

The operating move

Scale AI no faster than the organization can review, correct, and answer for its output. Budget oversight capacity as part of the operating model, not as invisible work added after deployment.
