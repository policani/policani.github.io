AI operating governance · Field note

AI adoption starts with the constraint, not the tool

Begin with the recurring workflow constraint, then choose the minimum intervention and proof needed to scale.

TL;DR

Begin with the recurring workflow constraint, then choose the minimum intervention and proof needed to scale.

What the paper develops

Most AI portfolios can show activity long before they can show value. This paper explains why tool-first adoption creates that gap and replaces it with a constraint-first operating model: identify the recurring workflow, name what limits value, compare AI with simpler interventions, and decide what evidence would justify more reliance. It connects experimentation to a five-gate portfolio cadence so that pilots become comparable investment decisions rather than isolated demonstrations.

The operating move

Before buying or piloting anything, name the recurring workflow constraint, compare AI with simpler interventions, assign an owner, and define the evidence that would justify another increment of investment.
