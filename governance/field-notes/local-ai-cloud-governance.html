AI operating governance · Field note

Where should this AI run?

TL;DR

A claims tool and a writing aid can need different answers. Compare local, cloud, and hybrid options against each workflow's data, access, actions, support, and exit needs.

What the paper develops

A claims team wants AI to help with sensitive records. One group favors a local model. Another offers a cloud service with stronger support. The leader cannot choose from model quality alone: neither offer yet says who may read the records, what AI may do, or who restores service when it fails.

A low-risk writing aid may need a different answer. Local, cloud, and hybrid are ways to run AI. Each has to meet the needs of the work it supports.

Check data, access, actions, changes, failures, operations, and supplier exit for each workflow. Have the workflow owner recommend an option. A leader with approval authority makes the choice, accepts the remaining risk within that authority, and records when the choice must be reviewed again.

What to do next

Run seven checks on two real workflows. Have the workflow owner recommend an option and name who can approve it and accept the remaining risk.
