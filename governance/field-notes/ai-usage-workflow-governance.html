AI operating governance · Field note

AI usage belongs in workflow governance, not blank-check access

Govern AI access where the work happens, proportioned to the stakes of each workflow.

Two-minute briefing A concise companion to the full white paper.

TL;DR

Govern AI access where the work happens, proportioned to the stakes of each workflow.

The operating move

Set AI permissions at the workflow level. Match access, review, and evidence requirements to the data involved, the consequence of error, and the ability to recover when the output is wrong.
