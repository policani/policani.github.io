Field note · Sep 2026

Why the kill gate doesn't fire

A committee with the power to stop a failing program often does not use it. The reason is who gets the decision. It usually goes back to the people who approved the program, and they are poorly placed to end it. If a program misses a target that was set at approval, let someone who had no part in that approval decide whether it continues.

AI Deployment Is a Jurisdiction Decision Before It Is a Model Decision

Set provisional limits on what work the AI may do, what data it may touch, and what it is allowed to act on. Do that before you compare vendors, then check the vendor you picked against those limits before you sign.

Felt productivity is not measured productivity

People can badly misjudge how much AI speeds them up. Judge the tool on what it delivered, measured against a baseline taken before the tool arrived, and keep self-reported speed in a record of its own.
