How to read this body of work

Each repository handles one leadership workflow. The value is in the boundary: what comes in, what gets clarified, what remains human-owned, and what moves downstream.

  • Signal before decision Clean up status, ownership, readiness, risk, and dependency evidence before executives act.
  • Governance without theater Use the smallest structure that creates visibility, decision quality, and follow-through.
  • AI with accountability Use AI for structure, synthesis, review, and repeatability. Keep approvals and risk with people.

Lifecycle map

From rough demand to governed execution.

The portfolio is designed as a modular operating chain. Each module has a narrow job and a clear handoff, so the system does not confuse idea review, business case, chartering, scoring, sequencing, execution governance, readiness, controls, and value tracking.

Clean the signal

Audit portfolio data, status, owners, sponsors, dates, duplicate demand, readiness, and KPI hygiene.

Classify intake

Route new demand to clarify, hold, reject, business case, charter, or portfolio scoring.

Shape investment

Build business cases, charters, scoring views, and governance formation artifacts.

Sequence commitments

Show capacity pressure, dependencies, milestone exposure, and executive tradeoff options.

Govern execution

Run review packs, operations logs, readiness gates, controls, ecosystem governance, and value realization.
