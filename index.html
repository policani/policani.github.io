Positioning

A portfolio of reusable operating systems, not a website module.

This site is the public front door for a collection of governance packages, prompts, templates, examples, and decision-support workflows. The website and GitHub profile are publishing layers. The modules are the reusable systems.

What it demonstrates: enterprise PMO judgment, portfolio decision discipline, executive cadence design, readiness controls, and practical human-governed AI.

Who it serves: operators, PMO leaders, portfolio leaders, program leaders, executives, consultants, recruiters, and hiring managers who need evidence of how complex work can be structured.

How to read this portfolio

Evaluate the work by the operating problems it makes visible.

PMO and portfolio leaders

Reusable systems for intake, prioritization, capacity, executive reporting, readiness, controls, and value tracking.

Program and project leaders

Practical artifacts for charters, business cases, launch gates, risks, decisions, dependencies, and sponsor-ready communication.

Executives and operating teams

Clearer decision views, escalation paths, tradeoff framing, capacity exposure, and follow-through discipline.

Recruiters and hiring managers

Evidence of how I structure ambiguous enterprise work, design governance systems, and apply AI without handing over accountability.

Operating chain

From rough demand to governed execution.

  1. Formation and intake: clarify sponsorship, decision rights, taxonomy, and readiness before work becomes false WIP.
  2. Investment logic and initiation: convert rough intent into business cases and charters with explicit assumptions, risks, benefits, and ownership.
  3. Portfolio decision support: score, sequence, review, escalate, and clean portfolio signal so leaders can make tradeoffs.
  4. Readiness, controls, and outcomes: assess launch readiness, exposure, evidence, and whether promised value materialized.
  5. AI governance and workflow systems: use AI for synthesis, review, drafting, classification, and repeatability while preserving human judgment.
